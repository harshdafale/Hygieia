Emergency protocol

Follow the steps in order

Medical emergency

Press the button to bring up the first actions.

First actions
  1. Call out for another crew member.
  2. Stay with the patient and keep them still and calm.
  3. Bring the medical kit.
  4. Contact the ground flight surgeon as soon as the link is available.
Chest pain
  1. Stop all activity and keep the patient calm.
  2. Follow the cardiac procedure in the onboard medical handbook.
  3. Monitor breathing and pulse until help is available.
  4. Contact the ground flight surgeon.
Difficulty breathing
  1. Check cabin alarms for smoke, CO2 and low oxygen.
  2. If the air is bad, move to clean air or use an emergency mask.
  3. Help the patient breathe slowly and stay calm.
  4. Alert the crew medical officer.
Severe bleeding
  1. Apply firm direct pressure with clean gauze.
  2. If blood soaks through, add gauze on top. Do not remove the first layer.
  3. Keep the pressure on while others bring help.
Burn
  1. Cool the area with a clean, cool, damp cloth for several minutes.
  2. Do not pop blisters. Cover loosely with a clean dressing.
  3. Large or deep burns are urgent. Alert the crew medical officer.
Head injury or loss of consciousness
  1. Keep the patient as still as possible.
  2. Check that they are breathing.
  3. Give no food or drink.
  4. Call for help and monitor closely.
AstroMed is a student prototype. It gives guidance, not a diagnosis, and does not replace a physician. Sample data only.
